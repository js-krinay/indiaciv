# IC-91 · Labelled Indian analogues

The [row ledger](IC-91-analogues.csv) covers all **261** IC-90 `not-comparable` rows in source order. **165** have a proposed fill and **96** are marked `cannot fill`. The `ic90_csv_row` column is the original physical line number including the header. Several upstream rows can use one Indian observation. For example, the three winter labels can reuse one cold-wave measure, and three software details point to one SHRUG workplace grouping. The counts are slot decisions.

The Directorate General of Maritime Administration's January 2026 report establishes a directed coastal origin–destination subset that IC-90 had missed. Its table 7 describes **FY 2024-25**. The atlas's separate April–October 2026 coastal tonnage remains a single-port observation. Preserve both. The report has 20 entries, several spanning multiple ports. The source can support named partners, documented mode, commodities present and relative volume bands. It cannot support measured route distance, all-mode shares or customs direction. [Coastal Shipping Committee report, §2.4 and table 7](https://dgma.gov.in/download/1769691524_697b598415456_coastal-committee-report-final-jan2026.pdf).

`Fill` means the source supports the proposed scope with the recorded guard. It can represent a narrow pilot or a shared observation. Where presentation assumes currency, exact quantities, a total or a benchmark, keep the original scalar empty and expose the analogue through the existing table, caption, detail or lens metadata. If the destination cannot render it accurately within the allowed caption and missing-value edits, keep that binding empty. Retain `period`, `vintage: "older"` and separate source/metric IDs. Apply the period to every display, including compact headers and tooltips. Treat `hide_rank` as a requirement to suppress ranks, rather than an assertion that this flag exists in upstream. For mixed periods, do not share a scale or ranking unless the audience can identify every observation's period and the comparison itself is valid. The ledger's periods before 2026 indicate the newest usable fallback verified for the scope stated, with no qualifying 2026 counterpart established in this review.

### Contract checks before implementation

- **City View.** Upstream `cityView/model.js` derives worker shares and LQ from counts. Compute India’s Census share from matched numerator and denominator and render the share as such. Keep residence-based workers separate from SHRUG non-farm workplace jobs. Use the pinned SHRIC concordance. Its group 72 pools several kinds of manufacturing, including defence-mixed classes already excluded by IndiaCiv. The group cannot establish separate semiconductor, car or aerospace workplace counts. B-18 can supply civilian resident-industry details where its NIC codes and denominators are published. That use excludes the existing restricted mixtures and does not qualify a workplace plaque. Keep a common sector basis for every GVA tick and LQ. The nominal GDP series remains available separately.
- **Households.** Use the UP pilot only in compatible UP geographies. No statewide average is carried down to an individual UA. Index points are not currency or house price levels. NFHS insurance describes households, TRAI measures subscriptions and JJM's denominator is rural households. Preserve their scales. Don't calculate rent burden or affordability by pairing these independently scoped sources.
- **Trade.** The upstream `trade.json` renderer uses `value_musd` for width, ordering and mode arithmetic, and labels weight in short tons. Do not insert metric tonnes, index bands or midpoint estimates into any of those fields. Keep present-day route geometry separate from historical port relationships. A straight-line distance is only the separation of two port points. Reported commodity presence can share a tag, but cannot give a top commodity share. Several legacy petrochemical labels refer to the same report tag. For new joins use the port identifier and shipping direction. Preserve multi-port groups and skip unresolved links. The separate AAI passenger and freight views use derived shares or year-over-year change because AAI requires permission to reproduce its tables.
- **Hazards, parks and military.** Keep observation grain and units on each record. An observed impact at Mon, a fire detection and a day with cold-wave conditions are separate series. None establishes monetary damages. The Kaziranga lower bound applies to one month at one park. Show national MoD allocations as fiscal heads, preserve pensions as their own head and keep separately dated held-strength cohorts in a list. Officially named city/civic points and directory provenance are sufficient for the permitted military fields. Those records cannot estimate local payroll or personnel.

## Verification scope

The work is research only. No map outputs were generated. The CSV join, dispositions, labels, guards and group counts were checked against the IC-90 ledger. The unit suite ran 393 tests with 12 skips and no failures. The full pipeline check and browser preview require generated inputs; neither ran for these two research documents. Source coverage is marked as conditional where a build must still extract new tables, verify joins or test that the existing surface can render a given metric through metadata.
