# IC-70 · Blank geographies in partial parity rows

The IC-60 ledger and the six specified deltas reproduce **831 rows, with 407 live, 140 partial and 284 other**. The companion CSV audits all 140 partial rows and proposes **103 fillable candidates**. It recommends retaining the blanks on **37 rows**. A fillable row has a named source or reproducible method for some of its coverage. Where the source-to-geography join still needs verification, the CSV records an upper bound or an unquantified subset. Repeated parity rows on different surfaces can share one observation.

The baseline build at 7969ef8 provides the masks. State abbreviations, UN UA IDs, Census district IDs and zero-based hex/link indices identify the blanks. Scene cover uses presence of a positive class sample, which reproduces the audit counts. The two original cluster-type masks use an inferred icon crosswalk because the Indian renderer stores named qualitative plaques. The active lens value uses the audited density lens. Silver production and the research campus inventory lack a geographic denominator, so those gaps are described by their unenumerated remainder.

## Engineering issues in priority order

1. **State and city economy and labour · 26 rows.** Start with MoSPI Annual PLFS 2025 Appendix A Tables 31–33. Table 31 provides all-age WPR for the 2026 employment projection, Table 32 provides the older 15+ CWS unemployment benchmark, and Table 33 provides rural, urban and combined CWS industry shares. Check the age universe and sample before extending each series. Apply the last published WPR to 2026 resident population as an explicitly labelled carry-forward assumption. No matching 2026 quarter figure was verified for the omitted jurisdictions. Then add Odisha's verified nominal GSVA, search other state directorate releases, and use the RBI historical tables to bridge only compatible sectors and base years.

2. **Hex and urban geography · 35 rows.** Begin with the 90 density and district gaps and the 200 age-lens gaps. Audit genuine district intersections for the district tooltip and use mid-2026 WorldPop over Indian land for density and median age where raster coverage passes. The existing build already borrows district IDs for 58 cells, and some remaining detached islands lack district overlap. Apply the same footprint matching process to the 131 UAs outside City View and the existing point-only profile. Malappuram's UN point lies inside GHSL centre 9230 assigned to Kozhikode. Use the historical constituent-town list as an independent control against settlement cells. Any two derived footprints must be disjoint, with ambiguous geography kept point-only. Zone and tab expansion depend on the cohort being extended.

3. **Mapped resource, transport and incident inventories · 42 rows.** Start with the verified civil airport locality, BESS specifications and non-major port split. Next audit OSM route relations and protected-area and monument geometries using the shared link/feature-to-atlas join. Add GEM resources only after a verified site or newly audited footprint yields a positive match. For incidents use IMD's monthly publications as leads and require a matching date, hazard and geography in the agency record. For clusters use district export plans as leads and verify the operating named cluster against the operator or state authority. This issue shares a feature join across several authoritative sources. Its independent pulls can land incrementally.

## Source evidence and limits

- **GSVA.** The Odisha Economic Survey confirms FY 2025–26 advance estimates at current prices, including 39.1% for services. Other jurisdictions require individual series checks. Grow sector amounts by compatible nominal FY 2025–26 national sector growth before normalising to shares. For unsupported bridges, use the latest observed sector shares with their published date. PIB identifies Lakshadweep and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu as GSDP non-compilers. [Odisha Economic Survey](https://assembly.odisha.gov.in/WriteReadData/News/OES%202026%20WEB%20UPLOAD.pdf) · [MoSPI compilation status](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2258718)
- **Ports.** Table 2.2.1 in the BPS gives port-specific FY 2024–25 coastal and overseas totals. The resulting coastal shares are about 39.1% in Krishnapatnam and 27.0% in Gangavaram. The verified September 2026 table aggregates by reporting board. “Mundra (Old)” is a separate reporting entity from the modern terminal. Preserve the existing cargo rank cohort and period. [BPS](https://shipmin.gov.in/sites/default/files/BPS%202024-25_compressed.pdf) · [September 2026 report](https://shipmin.gov.in/sites/default/files/Cargo%20handled%20at%20Non-Major%20Ports%20Sep%202026.pdf)
- **Battery and power.** Parliament's operating BESS annexure contains 17 reporting rows, including Rajnandgaon. Some rows combine sites or omit exact locations. SECI corroborates Rajnandgaon's 40 MW and 120 MWh ratings. CEA state allocation totals have a different scope from the physical locations of GEM generation assets, and the GEM capacity row remains blank absent a compatible licensed observation. [Ministry of Power annexure](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2291650&lang=1&reg=3) · [SECI projects](https://www.seci.co.in/own-projects)
- **Air and weather.** Malappuram and Madurai are the two air blanks. TNPCB/CPCB document three Madurai monitoring stations. Validate pollutant and same-period completeness before aggregating, with exact observation dates for any monthly fallback. IMD May 2026 documents thunderstorms, lightning and gales. Its combined casualty reporting cannot fill a particular incident's death or loss field. Historical flood reports may be shown separately with their actual event dates. [TNPCB archive](https://www.tnpcb.gov.in/airquality.php) · [CPCB Madurai plan](https://cpcb.nic.in/Actionplan/Madurai.pdf) · [IMD May weather summary](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/cmpg/Product/Monthly_Climate_Summary/Monthly_Clim_Summary_5_2026.pdf)
- **Other gaps.** Rural tap coverage lacks the qualifying denominator in these displayed state cells. NFHS-based MPI has a different poverty definition from the monetary nowcast. RBI's transaction HPI is restricted to covered markets. The veteran series requires public aggregate registration counts, with no comparable district series beyond the Himachal pilot verified here.
- **Geometry and classifications.** The published GHSL database uses a 2025 reference epoch. The Kerala DES lists Malappuram UA constituent towns in §14.4 for Census 2011. Both dates belong on the relevant inputs if used. Carry the original land-cover vintage even when the City View scene is drawn today. [JRC data catalogue](https://doi.org/10.2905/JRC.05RDPR0) · [Kerala DES](https://www.ecostat.kerala.gov.in/publication-detail/panchayat-level-statistics-2011--malappuram--district)

- **Sites and incidents.** IndiaCiv's `infrastructure.py`, `resources.py`, `wonders.py` and `lenses.py` build payloads for the layer tooltips, Explorer, advisors and City View. Keep the observation period per port or incident, and scope any ranks to a comparable same-period cohort. Apply the existing civilian publication gate and exclude operational military detail.
- **All surfaces.** In mixed Explorer columns, name periods in the header and label individual values. Older observations carry `vintage: "older"` and their actual period. Keep them out of 2026 growth and ranking cohorts. Show exact dates for the applicable reporting year. Mark modelled values “IndiaCiv estimate” with the period, and state the computation in the Almanac.
