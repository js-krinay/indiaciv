## Remaining high-value gaps

- **Monetary poverty and household finances.** Newer poverty reproduction needs HCES unit-data access. District modelled mean consumption does not identify a district headcount below a poverty line. The RBI Tendulkar series is a separate threshold and the standalone file did not return in this session. NITI MPI can inform a separately named deprivation lens. Local income, home values, rent and rent burden still require valid distributions and denominators. [World Bank replication package](https://reproducibility.worldbank.org/catalog/541) · [RBI Table 151](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=21248).
- **Health, wellbeing and connectivity.** NFHS-6 district compendiums use a guest-login workflow. NFHS-5 OGD and the NFHS-6 public national/state fact sheets use household coverage, while the parity metric is uninsured people. Report 596 gives a national benchmark. Distress, loneliness and a multi-input wellbeing composite require their own measures. State telecom service areas and household access do not supply comparable local fixed broadband counts. [NFHS release details](https://www.nfhsiips.in/nfhsuser/release-details.php) · [NFHS-5 OGD catalogue](https://data.gov.in/catalog/national-family-health-survey-5-nfhs-5-india-districts-factsheet-data-provisional) · [CMS Telecom 2025](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/publication_reports/CMST_report_m.pdf).
- **Economic detail and freight.** ASI and ASUSE do not jointly establish a complete local account or workplace payroll distribution. The DGCI&S interstate series is a useful partial mode-specific lead for future research. Its report download did not serve PDF bytes in this session.
