# IC-88 · Public defence data for IndiaCiv

The [row-by-row ledger](IC-88-military.csv) audits 65 IC-85 rows: 57 previously hidden and eight partial. Eight retain their 2026 registration coverage, one can take a dated national strength fallback, and 56 remain unfilled in the upstream schema. Of the 56, seven have a civic analogue that can use the existing Explorer or Almanac fields. The selection includes the Military layer and advisor, explicitly military-restricted rows, and veteran columns and lens. The three live Union budget rows stay outside the ledger. Confidence concerns the source-to-field match or the stated gap, and is qualified by the research cutoff of 28 November 2026.
