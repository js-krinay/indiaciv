## Estimate decisions and gaps

- **Household consumption.** The UP result is an official historical small-area estimate using Fay–Herriot models, including a spatial variant. It answers a local question that a flat allocation of state MPCE cannot. For a separate October 2026 fixed-basket cost estimate, multiply the baseline state/sector MPCE by the October 2026 CPI divided by the mean linked CPI across the entire survey period. Use matching geographic and sector indices, or explicitly label a national-sector CPI assumption. The result estimates the cost of maintaining baseline consumption at October prices and assumes an unchanged real basket. The linked survey-period series was not extracted and validated in this pass. The ledger uses the verified older values. Income, household rent burden and home values would need their own compatible inputs. [CPI methodology and back-series access](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/CPI/National_Metadata_Structure_for_CPI.pdf) · [October 2026 CPI](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/release_calendar/1794480152014_Press_Release_of_CPI_for_October_2026_.pdf)
- **Freight.** DGCI&S indexes rail, river and air for FY 2024-25 and a separate GSTN e-way bill report for FY 2023-24. All attempted current and older direct files returned the application HTML to anonymous curl. The indexed evidence confirms reporting by state/block and selected modes. It does not establish an anonymously fetchable, eligible bilateral matrix. If that becomes available, State/UT freight partners, same-universe quantity shares and geometric distances can use it. A geometry-only route supplies no shipment counts. A gravity matrix from outbound and inbound totals would require observed pairs for calibration and validation. Road consignment values and selected-mode tonnes have different units and denominators. Railways' FY 2025-26 originating loads are useful at railway-zone scale, and cannot estimate segment loads without network assignment inputs. [DGCI&S catalogue](https://dgciskol.gov.in/pub_inland.aspx) · [Railway FY 2025-26 freight release](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=287291)
- **Hazards.** CSE/DTE has Jan–Sep 2026 state weather days for selected hazards. Day counts are useful for a separately designed occurrence measure. The source does not establish independently identified disaster events, common rupee losses or public redistribution terms. Government releases report physical impacts and relief amounts. Alerts and satellite detections also have different denominators from disaster events. The earlier 2024 inundation raster has positive pixels and unclassified areas. Populations assigned to verified wet pixels may support a separately labelled detected-exposure count after permanent-water masking and an observation-coverage audit. These checks are needed before making a city exposure estimate. [CSE/DTE report](https://www.cseindia.org/content/downloadreports/13324) · [MHA impact tables](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2294241&lang=1&reg=1)
- **Local economic detail.** A sector GVA allocation based only on old SHRUG workplace jobs assumes identical value added per worker within an industry and across places. No local validation series was established. Employment counts also cannot recover suppressed GVA or a missing historical national benchmark. Public 2025 NIRF participant returns offer institution-level student strength and some sponsored-project receipts. Campus aggregation, accounting scope and period need validation before a city comparison. [NIRF FAQ](https://www.nirfindia.org/Home/Faqs) · [Example submission](https://www.nciindia.gov.in/images/pdf/Academic_Section/NIRF%20OVERALL%202025.pdf)
- **Access and rights.** A public PDF or an unauthenticated catalogue is evidence of access to the publication, with reuse subject to the publisher’s terms. The ledger’s candidate source periods do not imply valid data at the dropped row's geography. The OpenCity city HCES extracts were a lead. They cover selected districts, carry an unverified downstream public-domain claim, and do not establish an official direct survey estimate for a UA. [OpenCity extract catalogue](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/household-consumption-expenditure-survey-2023-24) · [MoSPI HCES design and metadata](https://microdata.gov.in/NADA/index.php/catalog/237/study-description)
